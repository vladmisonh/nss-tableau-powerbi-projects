\# Car Accidents Analytics 2015–2022 – Power BI Dashboard



This project analyzes traffic accident data from 2015 to 2022 using \*\*Power BI\*\*, focusing on trends in accident frequency, causes, conditions, and locations. The dashboard provides an interactive way to explore patterns, identify high-risk factors, and gain insights for potential safety improvements.



---



\## Project Overview



The dataset contains detailed records of car accidents with fields such as:



\* \*\*Date \& Year\*\* of accident

\* \*\*Accident Type\*\* (e.g., Hit and Run, Collision Type)

\* \*\*Number of Vehicles Involved\*\*

\* \*\*Number of Injuries\*\*

\* \*\*Illumination Conditions\*\* (Daylight, Dark, etc.)

\* \*\*Weather Conditions\*\* (Clear, Cloudy, Rain)

\* \*\*Harmful Event Type\*\*

\* \*\*Geographic Coordinates\*\*



This dashboard answers key questions like:



\* What is the total number of accidents and percentage involving hit-and-run?

\* How do accidents vary by year, month, and weekday?

\* What are the most common collision types?

\* How do weather and illumination conditions affect accident frequency?

\* Where are accident hotspots on the map?



---



\## Tools \& Technologies Used



\* \*\*Power BI\*\* – Dashboard creation, DAX measures, and interactive reporting

\* \*\*Microsoft Excel\*\* – Data cleaning and preprocessing

\* \*\*Map Visuals\*\* – Accident geolocation plotting

\* Data source: \*Traffic Accidents Dataset\* (Excel format)



---



\## Dashboard Features



1\. \*\*KPIs\*\* – Total accidents, hit-and-run percentage, and interactive slicers for number of vehicles and injuries

2\. \*\*Accidents Map\*\* – Visual representation of accident locations

3\. \*\*Time-Based Trends\*\* – Accidents by year, month, and weekday

4\. \*\*Top Categories\*\* –



&nbsp;  \* Top collision types

&nbsp;  \* Top illumination conditions

&nbsp;  \* Top weather conditions

&nbsp;  \* Top harmful event types

5\. \*\*Interactivity\*\* – Filters and slicers to drill down into specific data segments
