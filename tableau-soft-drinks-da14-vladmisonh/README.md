# Soft Drink Sales Analysis – Tableau Dashboard

This project visualizes sales performance for a variety of beverages, including soft drinks, coffee, tea, and alcoholic products, using data from [Kaggle’s Soft Drink Sales Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/prasadahirekar/soft-drink-sales).
The analysis was created in **Tableau** to track revenue, profit, product performance, and customer trends over time.

---

## Project Overview

The dataset includes transactional sales data with details such as:

* **Date** of sale
* **Product Category** (Soft Drink, Coffee, Tea, Alcoholic)
* **Company** (Coca-Cola, Dr. Pepper, Pepsi, etc.)
* **Product Name**
* **Revenue** and **Profit**
* **Units Sold**
* **Customer Name**
* **State** of purchase

Using Tableau, I developed an **interactive dashboard** to answer questions like:

* How has sales revenue changed over time?
* Which states generate the highest revenue?
* Which products are top sellers?
* Who are the most valuable customers?
* How do sales vary across different categories and companies?

---

## Tools & Technologies Used

* **Tableau Public** – Dashboard creation and interactive filtering
* **Microsoft Excel** – Initial data review and preparation
* Data source: Kaggle Soft Drink Sales Dataset ([link](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/prasadahirekar/soft-drink-sales))

---

## Dashboard Features

1. **KPIs at a Glance** – Total Revenue, Profit, Units Sold, and Total Customers
2. **Revenue by Month** – Time series view of sales performance
3. **Revenue by State** – Map visualization of state-wise revenue distribution
4. **Revenue by Product** – Horizontal bar chart ranking top-selling products
5. **Revenue by Customer** – Top customers contributing the most revenue
6. **Interactive Filters** – Filter by date range, category, company, and revenue range
